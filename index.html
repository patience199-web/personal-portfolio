About me

I am a Frontend Developer with 2 years of experience building responsive, user-centric web applications. Having spent my early career mastering the art of crafting seamless interfaces with React and Tailwind CSS, I’ve developed a keen eye for clean code, accessibility, and intuitive UX.

While my roots are in the frontend, I’m a firm believer that the best developers understand the entire ecosystem. I am currently expanding my expertise into back-end technologies specifically Node.js, Express, and PostgreSQL to better understand how data flows through an application.

My goal is to transition into a Full-Stack role where I can bridge the gap between elegant design and robust logic. I’m looking to leverage my frontend precision with a growing backend toolkit to build high-performance, end-to-end digital solutions from the ground up
